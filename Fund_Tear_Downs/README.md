# Empirical Fund Teardowns

This directory contains the output datasets generated by applying the S.A.F.E. (Sector-Adaptive Fundamental Engine) to the underlying constituent equities of major institutional ETFs and mutual funds.

These stress tests are designed to empirically validate the engine's algorithmic modifiers, economic logic, and downside-protection mechanics across different structural market environments.

## Dataset Index & Test Objectives

### 1. The Speculative Growth Test
**File:** `ARKK_Teardown.csv` (ARK Innovation ETF)
* **Objective:** Validate the "Burn-Rate Bypass" and terminal growth safety rails.
* **Result:** The engine successfully identified the structural detachment between market valuation and trailing cash flows, ruthlessly enforcing a 0.0% capital allocation (lockout) across cash-burning, hyper-growth constituents to protect portfolio capital.

### 2. The Heavy CapEx & Momentum Test
**File:** `SMH_Teardown.csv` (VanEck Semiconductor ETF)
* **Objective:** Test 3-Year Free Cash Flow smoothing across cyclical hardware supply chains while tracking 200-day SMA momentum extensions.
* **Result:** The engine rejected fundamentally strong but mathematically overvalued chip designers (0% DCF Win Rates due to maximum growth-rate caps), successfully routing maximum capital allocation (8.0%) to the underlying undervalued manufacturer (TSM) as the optimal risk-adjusted nexus play.

### 3. The Cyclical Value-Trap Test
**File:** `XOP_XME_Teardown.csv` (SPDR S&P Oil & Gas Exploration ETF)
* **Objective:** Validate the Dual-Hurdle filter's ability to detect peak-cycle commodity traps.
* **Result:** The engine differentiated between companies creating genuine economic value added ($\text{ROIC} > \text{WACC}$) like EOG and COP, while applying severe value-trap penalties to low-multiple basic materials companies bleeding relative momentum.

### 4. The Capital Structure Bypass Test
**File:** `VFAIX_VGSIX.csv` (Vanguard Financials & Real Estate)
* **Objective:** Test sector-specific metric waivers and structural debt exemptions.
* **Result:** Confirmed the engine does not penalize banks or REITs for standard operational leverage, properly evaluating them on their sector-specific step-functions rather than traditional industrial DCF metrics.

## How to Read the Output (Analyst Cheat Sheet)
When evaluating these CSVs, refer to the following primary columns to understand the engine's automated investment conclusions:
1. **Target Allocation (%):** The exact Quarter-Kelly wager. `0.0%` indicates a hard algorithm lockout.
2. **Master Grade / Conviction Score:** The final blended rating (0-100) combining fundamental health (S.A.F.E. Score), valuation probability (DCF Win Rate), and downside tail-risk (Sortino).
3. **MC DCF Win Rate:** The percentage of 1,000 stochastic Monte Carlo simulations where intrinsic cash-flow value exceeded the current market price.
4. **Passes Dual-Hurdle:** The automated check ensuring a company's Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) exceeds its Cost of Capital (WACC), featuring automated waiver tracking.
